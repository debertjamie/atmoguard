## ℹ️ About AtmoGuard

AtmoGuard is a local air quality monitoring and risk-awareness platform designed to help people understand the air conditions around them and take preventive action before health risks become serious. The system combines air-quality data with environmental context so users can monitor pollution levels, spot potential hazards, and make informed decisions in daily life.

Communities can track current pollution conditions, identify risky periods, and receive simple recommendations to reduce exposure. By turning air quality information into a clear, easy-to-understand experience, AtmoGuard supports healthier habits and better preparedness for environmental conditions that may affect public wellbeing.
